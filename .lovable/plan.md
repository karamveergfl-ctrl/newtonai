# Investor brochure refresh

## Outcome
- Deliver a new, print-ready A4 landscape PDF, leaving the earlier brochure untouched.
- Make the brochure feel like a concise investor presentation: clear headline, sharper tagline, fewer words, generous spacing, and readable type.
- Update the funding offer everywhere to **₹20 lakh for 5% equity**. Remove the old ₹70 lakh / 10% / ₹7 crore figures; do not invent a new valuation or unsupported performance claims.

## Pages
1. **Cover:** NewtonAI name, an investor-focused headline and concise tagline, one-sentence proposition, and the ₹20 lakh / 5% offer.
2. **Need + answer:** The fragmented learning/classroom problem and NewtonAI’s unified response, with a short visual workflow.
3. **Product + customers:** One clear, legible product screenshot and brief explanations of the student/teacher and institutional use cases.
4. **Model + ask:** Global learner subscriptions and India classroom plans, the investment terms, high-level use of funds, and website contact.

## Visual and production details
- Match NewtonAI’s existing teal, blue, amber, dark-ink palette and logo; use restrained graphic hierarchy instead of dense cards and paragraphs.
- Use A4 landscape dimensions, large headings and comfortably readable body type; keep all content inside print-safe margins.
- Reuse an existing product screenshot only where it remains legible at print size; omit tiny or redundant screenshots.
- Generate a versioned PDF in Files, render and inspect every page for clipping, overlap, weak contrast, tiny text, and incorrect figures, then correct any issues before delivery.
